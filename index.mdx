title: "Infinite Mac: Classic Macs in Your Browser"
sidebarTitle: "Home"
description: "Run classic Mac OS, NeXTSTEP, and early Mac OS X instantly in your browser — no downloads, no installation, powered by WebAssembly."
---

Infinite Mac brings classic Macintosh computers from the 1980s through the early 2000s to life directly in your web browser. Pick any system from the library — System 1 through Mac OS X 10.4 — and you're running in seconds, with a preloaded software library and optional CD-ROM access.
